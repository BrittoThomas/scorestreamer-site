ScoreStreamer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

ScoreStreamer ("the app", Android package com.britto.scorestreamer) is published by Britto Thomas. Contact: brittovadakkethala@gmail.com.

Summary

The app has no server of its own and no user accounts. The developer does not collect, store or sell personal data. Video you stream goes from your phone directly to your own YouTube channel.

What the app accesses on your phone

AccessWhyWhere it goes
Camera and microphone To film the match and its sound for the live stream. To YouTube when you go live. No video or audio is saved on the phone.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi) and the Location permission To find and connect your two phones (streamer and scoreboard). Android requires the Location permission for finding nearby devices. The app does not read, use or store your location. Only between your two phones.

Google and YouTube

If you sign in to YouTube, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • read your channel name and picture, to show which channel will be used;
  • create, start and end live broadcasts on your channel when you press Go Live and Stop.

The app requests the Google permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube for this. On your phone the app stores only your Google account email address and channel name, so that you stay signed in. It does not store your password. Access tokens are issued by Google on your phone and are not sent to the developer.

ScoreStreamer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

ScoreStreamer uses YouTube API Services. By using the streaming feature you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can remove the app's access at any time: tap Sign out in the app, or visit your Google security settings.

Scores and team names

Team labels and scores you enter are kept on the scoreboard phone and sent only to the streamer phone you connect, where they are drawn on the video.

Data the developer collects

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service, and nothing is sent to the developer.

Deleting your data

All app data is on your own phones. Signing out removes the stored Google account email and channel name; uninstalling the app removes everything else. Live streams stay on your YouTube channel until you delete them in YouTube.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

This page will be updated if the app's data practices change.
